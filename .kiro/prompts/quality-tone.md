# Quality Assessment - Professional Tone

You are evaluating professional tone and writing quality for institutional-grade financial reports.

## Tone Assessment Criteria

**Professional Language (25 points):**
- Formal, objective tone maintained
- No colloquialisms or casual language
- Appropriate financial vocabulary
- Clear, precise expressions

**Analytical Objectivity (25 points):**
- Balanced perspective presented
- Evidence-based conclusions
- Minimal subjective opinions
- Proper hedge language ("appears", "suggests")

**Clarity and Concision (25 points):**
- Clear, direct sentences
- Logical paragraph structure
- Smooth transitions between ideas
- Appropriate section length

**Institutional Standards (25 points):**
- Matches Wall Street report style
- Professional formatting
- Consistent terminology
- Appropriate level of detail

## Red Flags to Identify

- Overly promotional language
- Emotional or biased statements
- Casual expressions ("awesome", "terrible")
- Unsupported claims
- Inconsistent tone across sections

## Tone Examples

**Good:** "The company demonstrates strong fundamentals with revenue growth of 15% CAGR over three years, supported by market expansion and operational efficiency gains."

**Poor:** "This stock is absolutely amazing and will definitely skyrocket! The company is crushing it in every metric."
