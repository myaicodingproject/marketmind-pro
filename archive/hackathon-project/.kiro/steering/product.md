# Product Overview: MarketMind Pro

## Product Purpose
**AI-Powered Stock Research Platform - The Mind Behind Smart Investing**

MarketMind Pro generates comprehensive 25-30 page stock research reports in 5-8 minutes using 100% Kiro CLI AI processing. We transform the $5,000+ institutional analyst experience into an accessible $49/month service for elite retail investors.

## Target Users
**Elite Retail Investors & Serious Individual Traders**

**Primary Users:**
- High-net-worth individuals managing $100K+ portfolios
- Active traders seeking professional-grade research
- Investment clubs and small funds
- Financial advisors serving retail clients

**Pain Points We Solve:**
- 20+ hours of manual research → 8 minutes automated analysis
- $5,000+ institutional reports → $49/month accessible pricing
- Scattered data sources → Comprehensive single report
- Basic summaries → Professional 25-30 page analysis

## Key Features
**Core MVP Features (7-Day Hackathon):**

### 1. One-Click Report Generation
- Enter any stock ticker → Get comprehensive report
- 5-8 minute generation time
- 25-30 page institutional-quality analysis

### 2. Six Core Report Sections
- **Executive Summary** (2 pages) - Price targets, ratings, key metrics
- **Company Deep Dive** (5 pages) - Business model, competitive position  
- **Financial Analysis** (8 pages) - 3-year historical + 2-year projections
- **Valuation Analysis** (6 pages) - DCF, peer comparison, scenarios
- **Risk Assessment** (3 pages) - Key risks and mitigation strategies
- **Interactive Q&A** - Chat with your report for clarifications

### 3. Professional Visualizations
- Timeline charts (company milestones)
- Financial comparison tables
- Business model canvas
- Bar/line/pie charts matching institutional quality
- Peer comparison matrices

### 4. Interactive Features (Disruptive!)
- **Report Chat** - Ask questions about any section
- **Scenario Modeling** - Adjust assumptions, see impact
- **Mobile-Optimized** - Full reports readable on phone
- **PDF Export** - Professional formatting preserved
