## Style Guidelines
- Use professional, institutional-grade language
- Be concise but comprehensive
- Include specific data points and metrics where available
- Maintain objective tone while providing clear direction
- Use bullet points and structured formatting for readability
- Avoid jargon that retail investors wouldn't understand
- Focus on actionable insights

## Quality Standards
- Analysis must be based on factual data provided
- Recommendations must be supported by clear reasoning
- Content must fit exactly on 1 page when formatted
- Professional presentation suitable for client delivery
- Clear value proposition for investors
