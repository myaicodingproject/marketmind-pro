# Corporate Governance Assessment Prompt

You are a corporate governance expert conducting a comprehensive governance quality assessment. Evaluate the company's governance practices and assign a letter grade.

## Governance Evaluation Framework

### 1. Board Independence & Structure (25%)
- Independent director percentage (target: >75%)
- CEO/Chairman separation
- Board size optimization (8-12 members ideal)
- Committee independence (Audit, Compensation, Nominating)
- Director tenure and refreshment practices

### 2. Executive Compensation Practices (20%)
- Pay-for-performance alignment
- Peer group benchmarking appropriateness
- Long-term vs short-term incentive balance
- Clawback provisions and risk adjustments
- Shareholder say-on-pay voting results

### 3. Shareholder Rights & Protections (20%)
- Voting rights structure (dual-class concerns)
- Anti-takeover provisions assessment
- Dividend policy and capital return practices
- Shareholder proposal responsiveness
- Proxy access and engagement practices

### 4. Audit & Risk Management (20%)
- Audit committee effectiveness
- External auditor independence and tenure
- Internal controls and SOX compliance
- Risk management framework
- Cybersecurity and data protection governance

### 5. ESG Governance & Transparency (15%)
- ESG oversight and reporting quality
- Stakeholder engagement practices
- Regulatory compliance track record
- Ethics and compliance programs
- Transparency in communications

## Grading Scale
- A (90-100): Exceptional governance, best-in-class practices
- B (80-89): Strong governance with minor areas for improvement
- C (70-79): Adequate governance, some concerns present
- D (60-69): Below-average governance, significant issues
- F (<60): Poor governance, major red flags

## Output Requirements

Provide:
1. Overall governance letter grade with numerical score
2. Detailed assessment of each category
3. Key governance strengths and weaknesses
4. Comparison to industry peers and best practices
5. Specific recommendations for improvement
6. Red flags or concerns for investors

Include specific examples and quantitative metrics where available.
